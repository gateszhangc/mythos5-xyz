The architecture behind Mythos 5's capabilities

A combination of advanced training techniques, novel architectures, and rigorous safety testing enables Mythos 5 to perform at unprecedented levels.

Core Architecture

Mythos 5 builds on a fundamentally improved training methodology. The model's architecture is designed for both breadth of knowledge and depth of reasoning, with specialized pathways for long-horizon tasks.

Safety classifier network

Separate classifier systems detect potential misuse in real-time, routing sensitive queries to a secondary model while maintaining normal operation for the vast majority of requests.

Safety

Built-in safeguards for responsible deployment

Multi-layered safety architecture

Mythos 5 includes classifiers that detect potential misuse across cybersecurity, biology and chemistry, and distillation attempts. When flagged, requests are automatically handled by a secondary model instead. Early data shows over 95% of sessions involve no fallback at all.

Cybersecurity safeguards

Classifiers prevent the model from providing uplift in cyberattacks, covering both exploitation and offensive cyber tasks. External testing found Fable-grade safeguards to be the most robust of any model tested.

Biology and chemistry safeguards

For general use, biology and chemistry related requests fall back to a secondary model. A trusted access program enables legitimate biomedical researchers to access full capabilities.
